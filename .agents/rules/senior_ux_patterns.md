# Senior UX/UI Interaction Standards & Design Cheat Codes

## 1. Conversational Phrasing & Natural Identity
- **Address by Name**: Personalize greetings (e.g. "Good morning, Emily" vs "Welcome"). Name recognition reduces interaction friction.
- **Conversational Queries**: Replace dry database field titles with human conversational language (e.g. *"How long did you sleep last night?"* instead of *"Sleep Duration (Hours)"*).
- **Relatable Tone**: Natural, spoken language is processed significantly faster than corporate or mechanical phrasing.

## 2. F-Pattern Reading Flow & Control Placement
- **Left-Aligned Controls**: Position radio buttons, checkboxes, and status toggles on the left side of their corresponding labels.
- **Eye-Sweep Efficiency**: Left placement follows natural Western F-pattern reading order, minimizing extraneous eye saccades and reducing cognitive scan fatigue.
- **Instant Recognition**: Users register the selectable state before reading the descriptive label text.

## 3. Dynamic Emotional Feedback & Micro-Interactions
- **Multi-Vector Selected States**: When an option is active, mutate multiple properties: bold text weight, brand color shift, and enlarged emoji/icon scale.
- **Live Reactive Morphing**: Mutate companion emotional cues in real time as sliders or steppers move (e.g. emoji facial expressions shifting alongside numeric inputs).
- **Progress Delta Comparisons**: Anchor inputs against past performance (e.g. *"2 hours more than yesterday"* transforms raw data entry into a personal milestone).

## 4. Recognition Over Recall (Cognitive Offloading)
- **Faces & Logos Over Strings**: Always display real avatars, recipient photos, or brand logos alongside account names.
- **Intuitive Certainty**: Human visual cortex recognizes faces and logos 60,000× faster than alphanumeric identifiers (account numbers, IDs).
- **Accidental Error Elimination**: Visual confirmation drastically reduces high-consequence mistakes (e.g. wrong transfer, incorrect assignment).

## 5. Consequential Transparency & Impact Previews
- **Preview The After-State**: Always calculate and display the immediate consequence *before* transaction finalization (e.g. show *"Current Balance"* alongside projected *"New Balance"*).
- **Clear Account Routing**: Explicitly show source and destination entities with distinct cards to eliminate ambiguity.
- **Zero Surprises**: Transparency empowers user autonomy and eliminates post-action remorse.

## 6. Curated Selection Over Blank Text Input
- **Tap To Select**: Replace open-ended text fields with selectable preset chips (e.g. onboarding roles, job titles, frequencies).
- **Typo & Friction Elimination**: Manual typing introduces friction, spelling variations, and abandonment. Tapping presets is instantaneous.
- **Safe Fallback**: Always provide a structured "Other" choice with an expandable write-in input for non-standard entries.

## 7. The Junior vs Senior Distinction
- **Junior Approach**: Builds interfaces that are merely *functional* (plain forms, generic labels, dry tables).
- **Senior Approach**: Synthesizes *functionality + emotional engagement + cognitive ergonomics* (predicts user hesitation, reduces friction, guides focus effortlessly).
